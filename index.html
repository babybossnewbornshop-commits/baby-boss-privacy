Privacy Policy

Business: Baby Boss (Newborn Shop), Bangladesh
Facebook Page: facebook.com/BabyBossNewbornShop
Effective date: 21 October 2026

Baby Boss sells baby products through its Facebook Page. This policy explains what information we collect when you message our Page or place an order, and how we use it. Our Page uses an automated assistant to answer common questions and to take orders on our behalf.

Information we collect

  • Your Facebook profile name and the messages you send to our Page.
  • Order information you provide: your name, delivery address and mobile phone number, the product and quantity you order.
  • Basic conversation history, so we can follow up on your order or questions.

How we use this information

  • To answer your questions about products, prices, stock and delivery.
  • To process, confirm and deliver your orders, including sharing your name, address and phone number with our delivery courier.
  • To follow up about an order or an earlier conversation with us.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information to anyone.
  • We do not use your information for any purpose other than serving your order and conversation with Baby Boss.
  • We do not collect payment card or mobile banking credentials. Payment is handled by cash on delivery or directly between you and us outside this assistant.

Sharing

Your delivery details are shared only with the courier company that delivers your order. Messages are processed through Meta platforms (Facebook Messenger) under Meta's own privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

Order records are kept as long as needed for delivery, returns and business records. You can ask us at any time, through our Facebook Page inbox, to delete your conversation data and personal information, and we will delete it unless we must keep an order record for legal or accounting reasons.

Contact

For any privacy question or deletion request, message us at our Facebook Page: facebook.com/BabyBossNewbornShop
